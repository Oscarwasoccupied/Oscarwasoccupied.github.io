I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of California, Irvine, advised by [Prof. Aparna Chandramowlishwaran](https://www.eng.uci.edu/users/aparna-chandramowlishwaran). I am part of [HPC Forge](https://sites.uci.edu/hpcforge/).

My research focuses on **foundational, generative models for physics**, and more broadly on **scientific machine learning (SciML)** and AI for science. 

I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University in 2024 and my B.E. in Telecommunication Engineering from Xidian University in 2021.

See my [publications](/publications/) for papers at NeurIPS, INTERSPEECH, and work under review. In my spare time I enjoy endurance sports (rowing, cycling, hiking) and powerlifting.
